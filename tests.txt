# Tests

## Test 1 - Creator promotion studio and attribution [sanity]
Viewport: desktop (1280x800)
Covers: creator marketplace, showcase, promotion studio, original-shop attribution
Description: Verifies a creator can promote a product and create a shareable attributed promotion.
Steps:
1. Sign in as a Creator account
2. Open Creator Marketplace and select Promote product
3. Open Create promo card from My Showcase
4. Verify the preview shows the original product image, shop name, price, SHOP NOW and marketplace path
5. Copy the trackable affiliate link
Expected: The product appears in My Showcase, the promotion studio opens, and an attributed link is copied that sends customers to the original product page.

## Test 2 - Business affiliate controls
Viewport: desktop (1280x800)
Covers: business affiliate program, creator eligibility, commission visibility
Description: Verifies the business Seller Center exposes creator affiliate controls.
Steps:
1. Sign in as a Business account
2. Open Seller Center and select Affiliate
3. Verify creator eligibility and commission settings are visible
4. Save affiliate settings
Expected: Affiliate settings are visible and the save action confirms the configuration.

## Test 3 - Mobile creator sharing
Viewport: mobile (375x667)
Covers: responsive creator promotion, share-link fallback, visible attribution
Description: Verifies the promotion studio remains usable on mobile and explains the separate trackable link.
Steps:
1. Open Creator Marketplace on mobile and promote a product
2. Open Create promo card
3. Verify the card shows the original shop, price, SHOP NOW and marketplace path
4. Copy the trackable link
Expected: The creator can copy an attributed link and the customer destination remains the original product page.

## Test 1 - Creator account stays creator-only and opens a clean creator workspace [sanity]
Viewport: desktop
Covers: role persistence, creator-only account dashboard, creator dashboard navigation, removal of cross-role conversion options
Description: Verifies a creator account opens a focused creator experience rather than showing buyer/business role conversion cards.
Steps:
1. Sign in using a Creator account
2. Open Account
3. Open Creator Marketplace from the creator dashboard
Expected: Account shows Creator status, creator earnings/showcase tools, and no Become a Business, Sell on Marketplace, or Become a Customer actions.

## Test 2 - Creator adds a product to Showcase and shares the original-shop attribution link
Viewport: mobile
Covers: creator product promotion, creator showcase, original business ownership, affiliate attribution link, product deep link
Description: Verifies promoted products appear on the creator dashboard and the share action generates a link containing the product and creator attribution while landing on the original marketplace product page.
Steps:
1. Open Creator Marketplace
2. Select a verified product and tap Promote product
3. Open Account and view Products I'm promoting
4. Tap Share original-shop link
5. Open the generated link
Expected: The product appears in the creator showcase, the card identifies the original business as seller, and the shared URL contains product and creator attribution and opens that original product page.

## Test 3 - Business account stays business-only with a professional seller workspace

## Test 4 - Buyer account stays buyer-only with shopping tools
Viewport: mobile

## Test 5 - Creator can promote and unpromote products without changing merchant ownership
Viewport: desktop
Covers: promotion toggle, original seller labeling, creator commission display, merchant ownership boundary
Description: Verifies creator promotion changes the creator showcase state while the product continues to identify the original verified business.
Steps:
1. Open Creator Marketplace
2. Promote a product
3. Verify the product shows the merchant name and commission
4. Tap the promotion button again
Expected: The product is added to the showcase, continues to show the original seller, and can be removed from the creator's promotions without changing the merchant listing.
